# SEULA: 2-minute pitch and live demo script

Judging weights: Actionability 30 · Insight 25 · Data 20 · Execution 15 · Clarity 10.

## 0:00 The gap (Insight)
Landing page, section 1.
"Finnish men with lung cancer have 16.7% five-year survival. Sweden and Norway are at 27–28%. Only 27% of patients reach curative treatment. The delay starts before the referral: in some counties one in three people feel they can't get enough doctor time, and stage is recorded for only 42% of cancers, so nobody can even see the delay."

## 0:25 The idea, live (Execution + wow)
Show the QR code. Ask a judge to scan it.
"Anyone can do this, on their own phone, in four minutes. It comes with the booking SMS you already get. Validated questions, then say 'aaah' for a few seconds. The voice is analysed on the phone; nothing is uploaded."

## 0:55 The doctor's side (Actionability)
Open the Clinician view (the judge's own submission appears at the top as "live").
"The GP opens the morning list already triaged. Pekka, 67: persistent hoarseness, VHI-10 abnormal, CPPS below the published dysphonia cut-off, so ENT laryngoscopy. Every flag shows the guideline it comes from. One click copies the history into the EHR, or exports FHIR. G8 rides along in the referral. That's roadmap action 3.4.2 done for free."

## 1:20 Does it work? (Data quality)
Evidence page.
"The phone's voice engine matches Praat, the clinical reference tool: r 0.95 to 1.00. And we're honest about it: voice finds an abnormal larynx, not cancer. The questionnaire carries lung and colorectal cancer, using NICE NG12 and PLCOm2012."

## 1:40 What a county should do (Actionability)
Recommendations page.
"Six actions with owners and KPIs. Start in North Karelia, the county with the worst access, with a comparator county. Nurses order the 2-week test straight from a flag. Timestamps go monthly to the quality registry. And it pays for itself on doctor time alone: two minutes per visit is 6 FTE per 300,000 forms."

## Backup answers
- **"Isn't NG12 British?"** Yes. It's a stand-in until FICAN sets Finnish pathway criteria (3.1.2, 2026–28). The rules are swappable data.
- **"Voice evidence?"** It's validated against Praat on controlled voices, and the pilot measures AUC against laryngoscopy. Voice never escalates triage on its own.
- **"Elderly without smartphones?"** A nurse phone call uses the same questions. That was PUHELU's original model.
- **"Costs?"** Impact page: every assumption is a slider, and the pessimistic preset is one click away.
